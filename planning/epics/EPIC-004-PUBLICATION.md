# EPIC-004 — Publication

## Objective

Establish publication workflows that turn approved internal information into controlled outbound channel updates.

## Scope

- Define publication lifecycle language, states, and external channel contracts.
- Separate publication orchestration from provider-specific transport adapters.
- Define idempotency, retry, audit, and status-propagation requirements.
- Identify catalog, supplier, workflow, and AI contracts needed before publication actions occur.

## Future Capabilities

- Channel-specific publication adapters.
- Approval and scheduling workflows.
- Delivery status tracking and reconciliation.
- Event-driven publication requests and outcome notifications.
