# EPIC-001 — Catalog

## Objective

Establish the catalog bounded context as the authoritative home for product information and its lifecycle.

## Scope

- Define catalog language, ownership boundaries, and future public contracts.
- Prepare domain concepts for product identity, naming, classification, descriptive attributes, and media references.
- Define application-level use-case boundaries and repository ports without committing to persistence implementations.
- Identify catalog events that other modules may consume.

## Future Capabilities

- Product creation and controlled updates.
- Catalog validation and lifecycle management.
- Search, classification, and enrichment integration boundaries.
- Versioned, event-driven catalog changes for downstream consumers.
