# EPIC-003 — AI

## Objective

Establish a bounded, observable AI capability that assists domain workflows without becoming a source of ungoverned business state.

## Scope

- Define AI request, response, evaluation, and audit boundaries.
- Keep model and provider integrations behind explicit ports.
- Define human-review and confidence-handling requirements before automated action is introduced.
- Identify events and workflow contracts needed by catalog and publication capabilities.

## Future Capabilities

- Assisted product data enrichment and classification.
- Content drafting and structured extraction.
- Evaluation, traceability, and prompt/version governance.
- Provider substitution without domain coupling.
