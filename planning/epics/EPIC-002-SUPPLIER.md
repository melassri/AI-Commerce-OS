# EPIC-002 — Supplier

## Objective

Establish the supplier bounded context as the owner of supplier identities, sourcing relationships, and supplier-provided information.

## Scope

- Define supplier language, ownership boundaries, and public contracts.
- Prepare interfaces for supplier data, source records, availability signals, and provenance.
- Identify dependencies and events exchanged with catalog and publication workflows.
- Preserve provider-specific concerns behind infrastructure adapters.

## Future Capabilities

- Supplier onboarding and record management.
- Source catalog ingestion and synchronization boundaries.
- Availability, lead-time, and quality signal handling.
- Traceable supplier-to-product relationships.
